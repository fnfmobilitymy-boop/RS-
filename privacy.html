隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 4 日

本政策说明 RS ANTIQUE FURNITURE & REPAIR（SSM：201703113342 (SA0419148-T)）在你浏览本网站及通过我们提供的渠道联系时，如何处理个人资料。

1. 我们可能收到的资料

当你主动咨询时，你可能向我们提供姓名、联系方式、家具或空间照片、尺寸、地址、维修需求及沟通内容。请只提供与咨询相关的资料，避免上传不必要的身份证件、财务或其他敏感信息。

2. 网站表单如何运作

网站咨询表单在你的浏览器中整理信息。本网站没有设置接收或保存该表单的后台数据库。只有在你选择复制内容，或点击 WhatsApp 发送入口并自行发送后，相关内容才会通过你选择的渠道提供给我们。关闭页面前未发送的表单内容不会作为咨询提交给我们。

3. 资料的使用目的

我们使用你提供的资料回复咨询、评估家具状况、讨论材料和维修范围、准备报价、安排已确认的服务与收送、处理售后沟通，以及履行适用的记录保存义务。我们不会出售你的个人资料。

4. 本地储存与访问记录

网站使用浏览器本地储存记住你的中英文语言选择，并使用会话储存避免在同一次浏览中反复播放品牌开场或显示咨询提示。你可通过浏览器设置删除本地储存。我们没有在本网站加入广告追踪或访问分析脚本。网站托管服务商 GitHub Pages 及其基础设施可能为安全和提供服务处理 IP 地址、浏览器、请求时间等技术记录，具体处理适用其自身政策。

5. Google Maps、WhatsApp 与 Facebook

本网站嵌入 Google Maps 地图，并提供 WhatsApp、Facebook、FAQ2U 及电子邮件链接。地图加载或你使用这些服务时，第三方可能接收技术资料或使用其自身的 Cookie 与储存机制。通过 WhatsApp、Facebook 或邮箱发送的信息，由相应服务提供商按其政策处理。这些服务的资料处理可能发生在马来西亚境外。

7. 作品与客户评价

网站展示家具维修作品及客户分享截图。若你认为展示内容包含不应公开的个人资料，或希望我们检查、更正或移除与你有关的内容，请通过下方联系方式通知我们。我们会核实并处理请求。

Privacy policy

Effective: 4 November 2026

This policy explains how RS ANTIQUE FURNITURE & REPAIR (SSM: 201703113342 (SA0419148-T)) handles personal information when you browse this website or contact us through the channels provided.

1. Information we may receive

When you enquire, you may provide your name, contact details, furniture or room photos, dimensions, address, repair requirements and correspondence. Please provide only information relevant to your enquiry and avoid unnecessary identity, financial or other sensitive documents.

2. How the website form works

The enquiry form organises information in your browser. This website has no backend database receiving or storing the form. Information is shared through your chosen channel only if you copy it, or use the WhatsApp link and send the message yourself. Unsent form content is not submitted to us as an enquiry.

3. How we use information

We use information you provide to respond to enquiries, assess furniture, discuss materials and scope, prepare quotations, arrange confirmed services and collection or delivery, handle after-service communication, and meet applicable record-keeping requirements. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Local storage and technical records

The site uses browser local storage to remember your Chinese or English language preference and session storage to avoid repeatedly showing the brand introduction or enquiry reminder in the same browsing session. You can clear it in your browser settings. We have not added advertising trackers or website analytics scripts. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, and its infrastructure may process IP addresses, browser details, request times and other technical records to deliver and secure the service, under their own policies.

5. Google Maps, WhatsApp and Facebook

The site embeds Google Maps and links to WhatsApp, Facebook, FAQ2U and email. When the map loads or you use these services, third parties may receive technical information or use their own cookies and storage. Information sent through WhatsApp, Facebook or email is also handled by the relevant provider under its own policy. Processing may take place outside Malaysia.
