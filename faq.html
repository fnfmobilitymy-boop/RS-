基本问题

可以自己选择新的布料或皮革吗？+

可以与我们讨论材质、颜色、触感和使用需求。可选款式需按实际材料供应确认，也建议把家具照片与喜欢的风格一起发给我们。

所有类型的沙发都可以翻新吗？+

常见的单人、双人、三人、组合及古典沙发都可先提供照片评估。是否适合翻新，取决于骨架、结构、损坏程度与材料，不能只凭款式一概确认。

只换海绵，不更换布料，可以吗？+

若原有面料仍能使用、拆装后可以重新包覆，可讨论仅更新坐垫海绵或填充物。需要先检查面料、缝线及包覆方式。

宠物抓破或咬破的沙发可以修吗？+

可以先发送受损部位和整件家具的照片。我们会按破损范围讨论局部处理、面料更换或重新包覆；新的材料也不代表不会再次被宠物抓损。

维修沙发和买新沙发，哪一种更值得？+

可从骨架状况、原有品质、维修范围、预算及家具的纪念价值一起比较。若结构仍适合继续使用，翻新可能是值得考虑的方向；先确认维修方案和报价，再作决定。

老旧或古典沙发适合维修吗？+

可以先评估。我们会把原有轮廓、雕花木框、结构及软包状态一起考虑，讨论如何保留风格、更新触感。损坏严重或结构特殊的家具，需要进一步查看。

出租房的沙发也可以安排维修吗？+

欢迎提供家具照片、数量、所在地及期望完成时间。我们会根据实际状况讨论可行的维修范围和安排；如涉及房东或管理处许可，请先完成协调。

修好以后一定会像全新的一样吗？+

翻新可以明显改善面料、坐感和外观，但效果会受原有骨架、材料、维修范围及家具状况影响。我们会先沟通可达到的效果，不以所有旧家具都能完全变新作为承诺。

价格与付款

付款方式和付款安排是什么？+

付款方式、订金（如适用）、余款及付款时间，应以双方确认的报价或订单为准。确认维修前，请通过 WhatsApp 向我们核实具体安排。

沙发维修大概需要多少钱？+

费用取决于家具尺寸、材料选择、坐垫与填充物、结构状况及施工难度。收送或其他安排也需单独确认。请发送整体与损坏局部照片、尺寸和所在地，以便讨论报价。

海绵与坐垫

普通海绵和高密度海绵有什么区别？+

密度反映同体积海绵的材料含量，与支撑及耐用表现有关，但不等同于软硬度。选择时还应考虑海绵品质、厚度、回弹、坐感及使用方式。

高密度海绵一般可以用多久？+

使用寿命会随材料品质、使用频率、承重、环境和保养方式变化，不能只按“高密度”三个字给出固定年限。我们会结合实际用途讨论适合的选项。

日常使用适合哪一种海绵密度？+

需要结合家具用途、使用者的坐感偏好、坐垫厚度和支撑需求选择。欢迎描述目前坐垫的问题，我们会讨论适合的材料与软硬度方向。

可以只更换其中一个坐垫吗？+

若结构和包覆方式允许，可以评估单个坐垫的更新。不过新旧坐垫的回弹、软硬和面料色差可能不同，需先确认整体效果。

服务流程

一般翻新一张沙发需要多久？+

工期取决于维修范围、材料供应、家具数量及当时的安排。确认方案时，我们会与你核实预计完成时间；如有搬家或活动日期，请一开始说明。

可以先了解报价吗？是否免费？+

请先通过 WhatsApp 发送照片、尺寸与需求，了解报价流程。如需要查看实物、上门或特殊评估，请在安排前确认是否产生费用及具体范围。

可以安排上门查看吗？+

请先提供所在地、家具状况和需要评估的内容。能否安排上门、可到访范围、时间及相关费用，需要与我们确认。

有提供收取和送回家具吗？+

请提供地址、楼层、电梯或楼梯条件、家具尺寸与数量，以便确认收送是否适合安排。服务范围、搬运要求、费用与时间会按具体项目讨论。

保修与品质

维修有手工保修吗？+

是否适用保修、保修范围、期限与条件，应以确认的报价或订单条款为准。确认前可询问材料与手工分别涵盖什么，以及日常使用或磨损如何处理。

翻新后会像新沙发一样吗？+

更换材料与更新软包可以带来明显变化，但不会自动改变原有骨架的全部状况。我们会根据家具实际条件与你沟通外观、坐感和细节方面的期望。

可以保留我现在沙发的设计吗？+

可以讨论保留原有轮廓、缝线或配色方向。旧材料可能已停产，新旧面料也可能有色差；如无法完全一致，我们会与你讨论接近或协调的替代方案。

Everything You Need to Know

Can I choose the fabric or leather for my new sofa cover?+

We can discuss materials, colours, texture and how you use the furniture. Available options depend on current supply. Send photos of your furniture and the style you have in mind.

Can you reupholster all types of sofas?+

Send photos of armchairs, loveseats, three-seat, sectional or classic sofas for assessment. Suitability depends on the frame, structure, damage and materials; it cannot be confirmed by sofa style alone.

Can I change only the foam without reupholstering the fabric?+

If the existing covering is reusable and can be removed and refitted, renewing only the cushion foam or filling may be possible. The fabric, seams and upholstery construction need assessment first.

Can you fix a sofa scratched or torn by pets?+

Send close-up and whole-piece photos. Depending on the damage, we can discuss local repair, replacing panels or reupholstering. New material is not a guarantee against future pet damage.

Is sofa repair worth it compared to buying a new sofa?+

Compare frame condition, original quality, repair scope, budget and sentimental value. If the structure is worth keeping, refurbishment may be an option. Review the proposed work and quotation before deciding.

Is sofa repair suitable for old or antique sofas?+

They can be assessed. We consider the original shape, carved frame, structure and upholstery when discussing how to preserve character and renew comfort. Severe damage or unusual construction may need a closer inspection.

Do you offer sofa repair for rental properties?+

Share photos, quantity, location and your preferred timing. We can discuss a suitable repair scope and arrangements after assessing the furniture. Please coordinate any landlord or building-management permission first.

Will repaired sofas look brand new?+

Refurbishment can improve coverings, comfort and appearance, but the result depends on the frame, materials, scope and original condition. We discuss realistic expectations rather than promise that every old piece will become completely new.

Price & Payment Terms

What are your payment terms?+

Payment methods, any deposit, the balance and payment timing should be set out in the agreed quotation or order. Confirm the arrangements with us on WhatsApp before approving the work.

How much does sofa repair cost?+

Cost depends on dimensions, covering materials, cushioning, frame condition and the work involved. Collection, delivery or other arrangements also need confirmation. Send whole-piece and damage photos, dimensions and your location to discuss a quotation.

Foam & Cushion

What is the difference between standard foam and high-density foam?+

Density describes how much material is present in a given volume and can influence support and durability, but it is not the same as firmness. Quality, thickness, resilience, comfort and intended use also matter.

How long does high-density foam last?+

Lifespan varies with material quality, usage, load, environment and care. The label “high density” alone does not establish a fixed number of years. We discuss options around how the furniture will be used.

Which foam density is suitable for daily use?+

The choice depends on the furniture, preferred comfort, cushion thickness and support needs. Describe the problems with your current cushions so we can discuss suitable materials and firmness.

Can I replace only one cushion?+

A single cushion can be assessed if the construction and covering allow it. New and existing cushions may differ in resilience, firmness or colour, so the overall result should be discussed first.

Service Process

How long does a typical sofa reupholstery take?+

Timing depends on scope, material availability, quantity and current scheduling. Confirm the estimated completion time when agreeing the work, and tell us early about a move or other deadline.

Do you provide a free quotation?+

Start by sending photos, dimensions and requirements on WhatsApp to discuss the quotation process. If an in-person, on-site or special assessment is needed, confirm any fees and scope before arranging it.

Do you offer on-site inspection?+

Share your location, furniture condition and what needs assessment. Availability, service area, timing and any charges for a visit must be confirmed with us.

Do you provide pickup and delivery?+

Provide the address, floor, lift or stair access, furniture dimensions and quantity so collection and delivery can be considered. Coverage, handling requirements, charges and timing are discussed for each project.

Warranty & Quality

Do you provide a workmanship warranty?+

Any warranty, its scope, duration and conditions should be confirmed in the quotation or order. Before approving work, ask what applies to materials and workmanship and how normal use or wear is treated.

Will my repaired sofa look like new?+

New coverings and renewed upholstery can make a noticeable difference, but do not automatically change every aspect of the original frame. We discuss appearance, comfort and detail based on the actual piece.

Can you match my existing sofa design?+

We can discuss retaining the original shape, seams or colour direction. Older materials may be discontinued and new coverings may differ in colour. If an exact match is not available, we can discuss similar or complementary alternatives.
