OUR SERVICES

不只是换一层布，
是让家具重新好用。

RS ANTIQUE 提供家具翻新、换皮换布、坐垫与软包维修，并就室内搭配与空间设计提供咨询。具体维修方式需根据家具状况确认。

01 / SOFAS & RECLINERS

沙发与功能沙发

适用于单人、双人、三人及组合沙发的布艺或皮面翻新，以及功能沙发外部软包更新。

  • 换布、换皮与表面材料更新
  • 坐垫、靠垫及填充物状况评估与更新
  • 缝线、软包包覆及局部磨损处理
  • 可见骨架与支撑问题的检查与维修方向评估

02 / DINING & EVERYDAY CHAIRS

餐椅、办公椅与休闲椅

针对椅面开裂、布料老旧、坐感下降或软包破损，讨论合适的材料与修复范围。

  • 餐椅坐面与靠背换皮换布
  • 办公椅、电竞椅及高背椅软包翻新
  • 休闲椅、扶手椅与理发椅外部软包维修
  • 椅架松动及外观细节的维修评估

03 / MASSAGE CHAIR UPHOLSTERY

按摩椅外部软包翻新

针对皮面剥落、开裂或外部软包老化，评估包覆材料、坐面、靠背及腿部软包更新。

此项主要指外部材料与软包维修；电机、电路、按摩功能或其他机械故障，需要另行说明与评估，不包含在默认软包翻新范围内。

OUR SERVICES

MORE THAN NEW FABRIC.
A NEW FEEL FOR LIFE.

RS ANTIQUE provides furniture refurbishment, reupholstery, cushion and upholstery repairs, alongside interior coordination and design consultation. The repair method is confirmed after assessing the furniture.

01 / SOFAS & RECLINERS

Sofas and recliners

Fabric or leather renewal for armchairs, loveseats, three-seat and sectional sofas, including external upholstery on recliners.

  • Fabric, leather and covering-material replacement
  • Assessment and renewal of seat cushions, back cushions and padding
  • Seams, upholstery wrapping and localised wear
  • Assessment of visible frame and support problems
Send sofa photos for an enquiry ↗
02 / DINING & EVERYDAY CHAIRS

Dining, office and lounge chairs

We discuss materials and repair scope for cracked coverings, ageing fabrics, worn padding and damaged upholstery.

  • Dining-chair seat and backrest reupholstery
  • Office, gaming and high-back chair upholstery
  • External upholstery for lounge chairs, armchairs and barber chairs
  • Assessment of loose frames and visible details
Send chair photos for an enquiry ↗
03 / MASSAGE CHAIR UPHOLSTERY

Massage-chair upholstery

For peeling, cracked or ageing external upholstery, we assess covering materials and renewal of the seat, backrest and leg upholstery.

This service concerns external materials and upholstery. Motor, circuit, massage-function or other mechanical faults require separate discussion and assessment and are not included in the standard upholstery scope.

Classic and carved furniture

Refresh upholstery on classic sofas, chaise longues and armchairs while respecting the original character, colours and details.

  • Upholstery renewal for carved wood-frame furniture
  • Fabric or leather replacement for classic seating
  • Assessment of frame, finish and localised damage
